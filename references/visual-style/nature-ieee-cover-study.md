# Nature / IEEE Cover Style Study

These are source links and style notes used to guide the redesigned promotional figure. Do not copy the cover art into this repository.

## Source Links

| Source | Link | Useful Visual Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Nature cover archive | https://www.nature.com/nature/volumes | One dominant scientific object, high contrast, large negative space. |
| Nature Machine Intelligence archive | https://www.nature.com/natmachintell/volumes | Abstract AI systems rendered as luminous networks, circuits, agents, or geometric fields. |
| Nature Biomedical Engineering archive | https://www.nature.com/natbiomedeng/volumes | Biomedical objects with cinematic lighting and restrained annotation. |
| Scientific Reports article pages | https://www.nature.com/srep/ | Article visuals often use compact multi-panel figures; promotional art should distill one mechanism. |
| IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering current issue | https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=10 | Engineered clarity: signal processing, pipeline overlays, biomedical device motifs. |
| IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics current issue | https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/xpl/RecentIssue.jsp?punumber=6221020 | Clinical AI pipeline conventions, charts, reports, and biomedical signal panels. |

## Extracted Style Rules

| Rule | Application In New Cover |
|---|---|
| One hero object, not a collage | A translucent profile head with EEG nodes is the dominant object. |
| Use evidence as light | P300 impulses and token paths are drawn as luminous scientific traces. |
| Typography must be subordinate | Title is present, but scientific mechanism carries the composition. |
| Keep a dark editorial stage | Deep indigo background with cyan/violet/amber signal accents. |
| Make computation physical | Language prediction appears as a constellation seeded by neural signals. |

## Design Contract

The redesigned cover should read as:

`EEG event-related response -> intent selector -> local language manifold -> completed phrase`

It should feel closer to a Nature / IEEE biomedical cover or graphical abstract than a SaaS product hero.
